Resource Profile: MADO Creator

Official URL: https://profiles.ihe.net/RAD/MADO/StructureDefinition/MadoCreator Version: 1.0.0
Standards status: Trial-use Maturity Level: 1 Computable Name: MadoCreator

A profile for the Device resource that represents the creator of an FHIR Imaging Study Manifest. The primary goal is to communicate the fields:

  • type = MadoDeviceType#mado-creator "MADO Creator"
  • manufacturer
  • owner (Reference to MadoCreatorOrganization)

Usages:

  • Use this Profile: MADO FHIR Imaging Study Manifest Bundle
  • Refer to this Profile: MADO Composition, MADO MHD DocumentReference Profile for DICOM KOS Imaging Manifests and MADO MHD DocumentReference Profile for FHIR Imaging Study Manifest
  • Examples for this Profile: Device/MadoCreatorDeviceExample

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics
