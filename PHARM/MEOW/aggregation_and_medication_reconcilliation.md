## Aggregation and Medication Reconcilliation

This section discusses how medication information from different sources is aggregated into a medication overview, and how that overview supports medication reconciliation.

A medication overview can be assembled by aggregating medication information that already exists in a patient's records - typically prescriptions, dispenses, administrations and statements - and summarizing it into medication treatment lines. This aggregated view is a key input to medication reconciliation, where the medications a patient is actually taking are compared with what has been prescribed and dispensed, and discrepancies are identified and resolved.

### Medication List Types

Different kinds of medication lists arise depending on how and when they are produced - for example an active medication list, a discharge list, a reconciled list, or an aggregated view. A defined set of list-type codes is being developed by the expert group and will be provided, together with the corresponding ValueSet, in a future version.
